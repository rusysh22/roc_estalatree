1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They let the site remember your actions and preferences (such as staying signed in) and help us understand how the Platform is used. Similar technologies like browser local storage and tracking pixels serve comparable purposes.

2. Cookies we use

TypePurposeCan you turn it off?
Strictly necessary Sign-in sessions, security and CSRF protection, load balancing, remembering your cart, and honouring your cookie choices. No — the Platform will not work without them.
Preferences Remembering settings such as language or a dismissed notice. Yes.
Analytics Aggregated statistics on visits and usage so we can improve the Platform. Yes.
Seller storefront tags Some sellers add their own analytics or advertising tags (for example Google Analytics or a Meta Pixel) to their store pages. These are set by the seller as a separate controller. Yes — via your browser or the seller's own controls.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by service providers acting for us — for example our payment processor during checkout, or our analytics provider. Seller storefront tags are set by the relevant seller. We do not control third-party cookies; check the relevant provider's policy for details.

4. Managing cookies

  • Where we show a cookie banner or settings control, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies there and change your choice later.
  • Your browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break sign-in, checkout, and the cart.
  • You can opt out of many analytics and advertising cookies through your browser settings or industry opt-out tools.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as our use of these technologies changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows the current version.
