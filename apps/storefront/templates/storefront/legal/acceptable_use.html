1. General conduct

You must not use the Platform to do anything unlawful, harmful, deceptive, or that infringes someone else's rights. You must not help or encourage anyone else to do so.

2. Prohibited content and products

Do not list, sell, buy, upload, or distribute:

  • content that infringes copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, or other IP — including cracked software, unauthorised license keys, pirated media, and leaked databases;
  • malware, spyware, credential stealers, botnet tools, or instructions whose main purpose is to cause harm or gain unauthorised access;
  • stolen accounts, stolen payment data, fake documents, or tools for identity theft or fraud;
  • personal data of others offered without a lawful basis;
  • content that is child sexual abuse material, or that sexualises minors, in any form;
  • content that promotes terrorism, violent extremism, or human trafficking;
  • weapons, explosives, controlled drugs, or other items whose sale requires a licence the seller does not hold;
  • hate speech or content that harasses or incites violence against people based on a protected characteristic;
  • gambling services, financial or investment schemes, or “get rich quick” offers that are unlicensed or deceptive;
  • adult content that is illegal, non-consensual, or offered without required age controls;
  • academic cheating services, fake reviews, engagement or follower manipulation, and spam-generation tools;
  • anything else prohibited by Indonesian law or by the payment processor's rules.

3. Prohibited platform activity

  • circumventing fees, commissions, payment flows, or taking transactions off-platform to avoid buyer protection;
  • manipulating prices, reviews, coupons, referrals, or search ranking;
  • creating multiple or fake accounts, or using another person's account;
  • scraping, crawling, or bulk-downloading Platform data except as permitted; automated access without our permission;
  • probing, scanning, or testing the security of the Platform, or bypassing authentication, rate limits, or access controls, without written authorisation;
  • uploading viruses or code that disrupts the Platform; denial-of-service activity;
  • sending unsolicited marketing to other users; misusing the contact or support channels;
  • false, misleading, or exaggerated Product descriptions, fake “original price” claims, or bait-and-switch listings;
  • impersonating Berlanggan, its staff, or another person or business.

4. Buyer responsibilities

  • use purchased Products only within their licence terms; do not share or resell keys and access;
  • do not file dishonest refund requests or chargebacks for Products you received and used as described;
  • keep your account credentials secure.

5. Seller responsibilities

  • only sell Products you have the legal right to sell;
  • describe Products accurately, deliver promptly, and provide the support you promise;
  • keep prohibited content out of your store and your files;
  • handle buyer data only to fulfil and support orders, in line with the Privacy Policy and the Seller Agreement;
  • respond to Berlanggan requests about a listing or a dispute within a reasonable time.

6. Reporting a violation

To report content or behaviour that breaks this Policy, email abuse@berlanggan.com with the URL, a description, and any evidence. For copyright complaints, see Terms of Service section 8.

7. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and take any action we consider appropriate, including removing content, limiting features, holding funds or payouts related to the violation, suspending or terminating accounts, and cooperating with law enforcement. We aim to act proportionately and, where practical, to give notice and a chance to respond — but we may act immediately where there is a risk of harm, fraud, or legal exposure.
