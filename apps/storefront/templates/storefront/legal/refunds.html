This Refund Policy explains when a purchase made on Berlanggan can be refunded, how refunds are paid, and how to request one. It forms part of the Terms of Service and applies to every order unless a seller's Product page offers more generous terms.

1. Digital goods — the general rule

Products on Berlanggan are digital and are delivered and consumed immediately. Because of this, all sales are final once the Product has been delivered and you do not have a general right to change your mind. The exceptions below still apply, and nothing in this Policy limits rights you have under mandatory consumer law.

2. When you are entitled to a refund

You can request a refund within 7 days of the order date if any of the following applies:

  • Not delivered — you did not receive the license key, download, or access, and the seller has not resolved it within a reasonable time after you contacted them.
  • Defective or broken — the Product does not work, is corrupted, or cannot be accessed, and the seller cannot fix it.
  • Materially not as described — the Product is significantly different from what the Product page and plan stated at the time of purchase.
  • Duplicate charge — you were charged more than once for the same order due to a technical error.
  • Unauthorised transaction — the purchase was made without your authorisation (we may ask you to verify this and may involve the payment processor).

3. When a refund will not be given

  • You changed your mind, no longer need the Product, or bought it by mistake, after it was delivered and worked as described.
  • You do not have the technical setup, skills, or third-party accounts the Product page clearly said were required.
  • The request is made more than 7 days after the order date (unless mandatory law says otherwise).
  • The Product has been substantially used, downloaded in full, or its license key or access has been redeemed, activated, or shared.
  • Abuse of this Policy, such as repeated buy-and-refund patterns.
  • Wallet top-ups that have not been spent — these are covered by section 5, not this section.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are credited to your Berlanggan wallet as closed-loop balance, not returned to your original payment method, unless:

  • mandatory law requires a refund to the original method; or
  • the charge was a duplicate or unauthorised transaction, in which case we work with the payment processor to reverse it to the original method where possible.

Wallet balance from a refund can be used for any future purchase on the Platform. It cannot be withdrawn or transferred (see Terms section 3). Payment-processor fees are non-refundable.

For direct-payment orders (for example static QRIS paid straight to the seller), the seller is responsible for making the refund directly; Berlanggan can help mediate but does not hold those funds.

5. Unspent wallet top-ups

If you topped up your wallet and have not spent the balance, you may request a return of the unspent amount to your original payment method within 14 days of the top-up, less any non-refundable processing fee. After that, top-ups remain as closed-loop balance and are non-refundable, except where mandatory law provides otherwise.

6. Subscriptions

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals. The current paid period is not refunded unless the Product failed under section 2. If a renewal charge was taken after you cancelled auto-renew, contact us within 7 days for a refund of that renewal.

7. How to request a refund

  1. Contact the seller first through your order page — most issues are delivery or setup problems the seller can fix quickly.
  2. If the seller does not resolve it within 3 days, open a refund request from the order page or email support@berlanggan.com with your order number and a description of the problem (screenshots help).
  3. We review the request, ask the seller for their side, and decide within a reasonable time, usually 5 business days. Our decision on platform-mediated refunds is final.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before starting a chargeback with your bank or payment provider — we can usually resolve issues faster. Filing a chargeback for an order you received and used as described may lead to suspension of your account and wallet balance pending resolution.
