This Privacy Policy explains how Berlanggan (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use the Berlanggan marketplace and related services (the “Platform”). It applies to buyers, sellers, and visitors. For the meaning of terms not defined here, see the Terms of Service.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Berlanggan is the data controller for personal data processed through the Platform. If you are a buyer of a seller's Product, that seller is a separate controller for the limited order data we share with them so they can deliver and support your purchase (see section 4).

Contact: privacy@berlanggan.com.

2. What we collect

You give us

  • Account data — email address, password (hashed), and, if you add them, name, WhatsApp number, and notification preferences.
  • Seller data — store name, logo, bio, payout bank details, tax identifiers (for example NPWP) where required for invoicing, and any KYC information you submit.
  • Transaction data — the Products you buy or sell, order amounts, coupons used, wallet top-ups, answers to seller checkout questions, and payment-proof images you upload for manually-confirmed payments.
  • Support and communications — messages you send us or a seller, reviews, and contact-form submissions.

We collect automatically

  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page, and timestamps.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see the Cookie Policy.

From third parties

  • Payment processor — payment status, and a masked reference for the method used. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Sign-in providers — if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google.

4. When we share data

  • With sellers — when you buy a Product, the seller receives your email, the order details, your checkout answers, and any payment proof, so they can deliver and support the Product. Sellers must use this only to fulfil and support your order.
  • With buyers — sellers' store name and public profile are visible to buyers; a seller's private payout and KYC data are never shown to buyers.
  • Service providers — payment processing, hosting, email and WhatsApp delivery, error monitoring, and analytics, acting on our instructions under contract.
  • Legal and safety — authorities, our advisers, or other parties where required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Berlanggan, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — a successor entity in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Operator and staff access

Berlanggan staff and administrators may access personal data only where needed to run the Platform — for support, dispute resolution, fraud and security review, and legal compliance. Access is limited to authorised personnel, logged, and subject to confidentiality obligations. Admin actions on accounts, orders, and payouts are recorded.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside Indonesia. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as contractual protections) to keep your data protected to a standard consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data — while your account is active, then deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period after closure.
  • Transaction, invoice, and tax records — retained for the period required by Indonesian accounting and tax law (generally up to 10 years).
  • Support messages — up to 3 years after the last contact.
  • Fraud and security logs — as long as needed for the purpose, then deleted.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data (much of it is editable in your dashboard);
  • delete your data, or close your account — note we may keep records we are legally required to retain;
  • restrict or object to certain processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent for promotional messages at any time, using the unsubscribe link or your notification settings;
  • receive certain data in a portable format.

To exercise a right, email privacy@berlanggan.com. We may need to verify your identity. You also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk — encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls, and monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if a breach affects your data and the law requires it, we will notify you and the authorities.

10. Children

The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be notified by email or an in-Platform notice before they take effect. The effective date at the top of this page always shows the current version.
